    Roadmap to Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies, no difficulty setting, no mission medals. Cheats disable trophies and saving — never use them on your trophy save. There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves) and rotate the other 5.

    100% Completion — what it actually asks for

    Closer to 60% true completion: any 100 optional missions, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 of 397 stunts, all 8 bank jobs, all 39 gang operations, all shooting ranges and drone races, 15 of 18 meditations. Progress made during the story already counts.

    Missables & Warnings

    Only one trophy is missable, but a few things are time-sensitive. Everything else stays available in free-roam after the story.

    Save-slot plan

    15 manual slots + 1 autosave. Keep one save at the start of each chapter and one just before the final mission, and rotate the rest for free-roam cleanup. Never use cheats on this save — they disable trophies and saving.

    Time-sensitive, but not trophy-locking

    Race Against Yago in Chapter 3 carries an hourglass icon and can fail if you leave it too long — it feeds the Strangers & Freaks strand, so save first, then race the moment it appears. Some optional missions and fences disappear over time, but none of them gate a trophy.
